# Clinical review policy for exercise content

AS Care is a self-monitoring/education companion, not a prescribing tool. Current exercise text remains legacy reference material until reviewed.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Current axSpA-specific guidelines/consensus (NICE NG65; ASAS–EULAR).
2. Reputable national axSpA organisations and clinician-authored physiotherapy resources (e.g. NASS).
3. Peer-reviewed trials/systematic reviews for the specific movement/programme.
4. Generic musculoskeletal sources only as secondary technique references.

## Required record per exercise

- Clinical category: mobility, posture, extension, breathing, strengthening, aerobic, balance/other.
- Exact source, organisation/authors, date, URL and recommendation/page/section.
- Direct support for exact exercise vs broad category only.
- Starting position, movement sequence, target area and controlled range.
- Precautions, modifications and stop criteria; clinician review for disease-specific risks.
- Dose: holds, repetitions, sets, sides, rest and transitions, each with source.
- Reviewer role/name, date and status: NOT REVIEWED / REVIEWED WITH CHANGES / APPROVED FOR APP DISPLAY.

Do not infer dose from animation loop length, invent repetitions/holds/progression/contraindications, or imply every exercise suits every person with axSpA. AI review and automated tests are not clinical approval. Clinical and software statuses are independent.
