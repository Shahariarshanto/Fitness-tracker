# axSpA exercise clinical references

These are disease-level programme references, not blanket approval of every app exercise or dose.

## NICE NG65

Recommendation 1.5.1 recommends referral to a specialist physiotherapist for an individualised structured programme including stretching, strengthening, postural exercises, deep breathing, spinal extension, lumbar/thoracic/cervical range of motion and aerobic exercise.

Official: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/NG65/chapter/Recommendations

It does not prescribe the exact 24 app exercises or their repetitions/seconds.

## ASAS–EULAR 2022 update

Recommendation 4: educate people with axSpA, encourage regular exercise and consider physiotherapy. The task force notes study heterogeneity and limitations that prevent a definitive conclusion about which specific exercises are best.

Ramiro S, et al. Ann Rheum Dis. 2023;82:19–34. doi:10.1136/ard-2022-223296. https://ard.bmj.com/content/82/1/19

## NASS patient information

Typical programme domains include stretching, mobility, strengthening/posture and suitable cardiovascular activity: https://nass.co.uk/managing-my-as/exercise/physiotherapy/

Patient education is not individual clinical assessment.

## Citation rules

Cite exact recommendation, date and URL. Distinguish broad programme guidance from direct evidence for a named movement. Do not present a general recommendation as validation of an individual exercise.
