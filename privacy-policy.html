Privacy Policy

AS Care · Effective date: November 17, 2026 · Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AS Care ("the App") handles information when you use the AS Care web application at shahariarshanto.github.io/Fitness-tracker. AS Care is a personal exercise, wellness and health self-monitoring tool.

In brief: Cloudflare D1 is the primary data store. The App also keeps a temporary browser cache and pending offline edits. The current single-user API has no login or access control; anyone who directly accesses the public Worker endpoint may be able to read or change stored data.
  • Exercise and movement completion, goals and activity history;
  • Self-reported pain, symptoms, stiffness, fatigue and sleep information;
  • Medication names or schedule labels and adherence checkmarks;
  • Health measurements and laboratory values that you choose to enter, such as CRP, ESR, haemoglobin, ALT and creatinine;
  • Personal dates, notes and other information you choose to record in the App.

This information may be sensitive health-related information. Do not enter information you do not want stored in the Cloudflare D1 database.

2. Where information is stored

Cloudflare D1 (primary storage): The App sends the complete AS Care state to a Cloudflare Worker, which stores it in a D1 database. The App loads the latest database state when it starts and automatically sends changes after a short delay.

Backups: No R2 bucket or scheduled cloud backup is configured. The App provides a manual JSON export; you should periodically download and keep a copy separately. D1 is the primary database, not an independent backup.

On your device: The browser keeps a temporary cache and a pending-change marker so edits can be retried after an interruption. D1 remains the primary source when available.

3. Access and security

At the user's request, the current single-user API has no login, password or authentication. The Worker restricts browser CORS to the GitHub Pages origin, but CORS is not authentication and does not block direct API requests. Therefore, the health information stored in D1 must not be considered private or protected from third-party access. Do not store information here that requires confidentiality unless an access-control layer is added.

Cloudflare provides the infrastructure and is subject to its own applicable terms and privacy policy. The App does not use Google Drive or Google OAuth for data storage.

4. How information is used

Your entries are used within the App to display your activity history, progress, symptom summaries and other features you request, and to load and synchronize your data with the Cloudflare D1 database. The App operator does not sell your personal information or use your health entries for advertising. The App does not provide your entries to the App operator for profiling or independent analysis.

5. Sharing and third-party services

Your Drive data file is stored in the Google Account you select and is subject to Google's service terms and privacy practices. The website also loads Google Identity Services for sign-in and Google Fonts for typography. Those services may receive technical information such as browser and network data when your browser connects to them; their handling of that information is governed by their respective policies. The App operator does not otherwise disclose your entries to third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Temporary local cache: remains in your browser until you clear the website's storage or remove/reset the browser data. Pending edits not yet uploaded to Drive may be lost if you clear it.

To remove cloud data, use the relevant Cloudflare database controls. Export your data before making destructive changes.

7. Security

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device, avoid shared or public devices, and keep downloaded JSON exports in a safe location because they may contain sensitive health information.

8. Children

AS Care is not designed specifically for children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission from a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The revised version will be published at this same URL with an updated date. Continued use after an update means you have had the opportunity to review the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the AS Care project maintainer through the AS Care GitHub Issues page. Please do not post medical records, personal health details or other sensitive information in a public issue.
