Privacy Policy

AS Care · Effective date: November 16, 2026 · Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AS Care ("the App") handles information when you use the AS Care web application at shahariarshanto.github.io/Fitness-tracker. AS Care is a personal exercise, wellness and health self-monitoring tool.

In brief: Your entries are stored in your browser on your device. If you choose to connect Google Drive backup, the App transfers your backup file to your own Google Drive account. The App operator does not receive or store your health entries on an operator-controlled server.

1. Information you enter

Depending on the features you use, information stored by the App may include:

  • Exercise and movement completion, goals and activity history;
  • Self-reported pain, symptoms, stiffness, fatigue and sleep information;
  • Medication names or schedule labels and adherence checkmarks;
  • Health measurements and laboratory values that you choose to enter, such as CRP, ESR, haemoglobin, ALT and creatinine;
  • Personal dates, notes and other information you choose to record in the App.

This information may be sensitive health-related information. Do not enter information you do not want stored on your device or in a Google Drive backup you control.

2. Where information is stored

On your device: The App stores its working data in your web browser's local storage. The App operator does not have access to this browser storage. Data may be lost if you clear site data, uninstall/reset your browser, lose your device or use private browsing.

Google Drive backup (optional): If you select "Connect your Google Account", the App uses Google OAuth and the Google Drive API to create or update a JSON backup in a folder named AS Care Backups in the Google Account you authorize. The backup can contain the App data described above. The App uses the Google Drive drive.file permission, which is intended to limit access to files created by or opened with the App. You can choose not to connect Drive.

The App is a static website and does not currently operate an App-owned backend that receives, stores or analyses your personal entries. The App operator cannot retrieve your browser data or your private Drive backup through an operator account.

3. Google account data and permissions

When you connect Google Drive, Google provides the App with a temporary access token in your browser. The token is held in memory and is not intentionally saved in browser local storage or sent to the App operator. The App uses it to find or create its backup folder/file, read that backup when checking for a newer copy, and upload or update the backup when you request or enable syncing.

The App does not ask for your Google password. It does not request broad access to all files in your Drive, Gmail, Contacts or Calendar. Google handles sign-in, authorization and its services under Google's own terms and privacy policies. Review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. How information is used

Your entries are used within the App to display your activity history, progress, symptom summaries and other features you request, and to create/restore a backup when you use Google Drive. The App operator does not sell your personal information or use your health entries for advertising. The App does not provide your entries to the App operator for profiling or independent analysis.

5. Sharing and third-party services

Your optional Drive backup is stored in the Google Account you select and is subject to Google's service terms and privacy practices. The website also loads Google Identity Services for sign-in and Google Fonts for typography. Those services may receive technical information such as browser and network data when your browser connects to them; their handling of that information is governed by their respective policies. The App operator does not otherwise disclose your entries to third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local data: remains in your browser until you delete it, clear the website's storage or remove/reset the browser data.
  • Drive backup: remains in your Google Drive until you delete the backup file or folder from Drive. Disconnecting the App or revoking Google access stops future access but does not delete an existing backup.
  • Downloaded JSON files: are saved wherever you choose to store them; manage or delete those copies yourself.

To stop future Drive access, use "Disconnect" in the App and, if desired, review or revoke the App's access in your Google Account security settings. To delete a Drive backup, delete the AS Care Backups folder or its backup file directly in Google Drive.

7. Security

The App uses Google's OAuth authorization flow and limits Drive access to the drive.file scope. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device and Google Account with a screen lock and strong account security. Avoid using the App on shared or public devices, and do not share downloaded backups because they may contain sensitive health information.

8. Children

AS Care is not designed specifically for children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission from a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The revised version will be published at this same URL with an updated date. Continued use after an update means you have had the opportunity to review the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the AS Care project maintainer through the AS Care GitHub Issues page. Please do not post medical records, personal health details or other sensitive information in a public issue.
